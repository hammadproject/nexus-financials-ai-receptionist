Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from directors and finance teams before they sign up.

What services does Nexus Financials provide?

We provide outsourced payroll, bookkeeping, cashflow management, management accounts, credit control and tax records support, as well as back-office support for accounting firms.

Who will be handling our books?

You get a dedicated bookkeeper who works with your business directly and comes to know how it runs, rather than a rotating queue of tickets.

How is pricing structured?

Pricing is fixed and agreed monthly before we start, so there are no surprise invoices at the end of the month.

What software do you work with?

We work on cloud-based accounting systems, so your numbers are visible to you in real time rather than only at month-end.

Do you work with businesses outside Australia?

Yes. Our services are delivered remotely and we support clients worldwide.

Can you support our accounting firm rather than a single business?

Yes, we offer dedicated support services for accounting firms looking to outsource bookkeeping, payroll or reporting workload. See our Services for Accounting Firms page for details.

How do we switch from our current bookkeeper or accountant?

Our process is built to survive a change in accountants, software or business scale, so switching over is straightforward and handled as part of onboarding.

How do we get started?

Book a free consultation through our contact page and we'll get back to you within one working day to discuss your business needs.

Still have a question?

Get in touch and we'll answer it directly, no script, no pressure.

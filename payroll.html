Overview

Payroll is unforgiving: one missed filing or one wrong payslip and it's your team's trust on the line. We run payroll as a disciplined, dated process, not a task squeezed in between everything else.

Who it's for

Best for businesses with staff on the books who want payroll off their desk entirely — from a five-person team to a multi-site operation.

What's included

  • Weekly, fortnightly or monthly payroll runs, calculated and checked before release

  • Statutory deductions, filings and year-end submissions handled and logged

  • Payslips issued directly to employees through a secure portal

  • New hire onboarding and termination processing, including contract and entitlement changes

  • Superannuation contributions calculated and reported on time

  • A single point of contact for employee payroll queries
